About Us

Where Quality Meets Care

Amman Fabrication & Roofings

     Amman Fabrication and Roofing specializes in high-quality roofing sheets, steel structures, and PEB solutions for industrial, commercial, and residential projects. With advanced machinery and skilled workmanship, we deliver reliable roofing, cladding, purlins, and structural fabrication with precision and efficiency.

    We manage manufacturing, fabrication, and installation under one roof, ensuring consistent quality, safe execution, and timely project completion. Our focus on durable materials, industry standards, and customer satisfaction makes us a dependable partner for roofing and structural solutions.

Contact Us

Our Vision

To become a trusted and recognized leader in the roofing and structural fabrication industry by consistently delivering high-quality, durable, and innovative solutions. We aim to set industry standards through reliability, timely execution, and long-term performance while contributing to stronger and safer infrastructure development.

Our Mission

To provide strong, reliable, and cost-effective roofing and fabrication services through advanced manufacturing technology and skilled workmanship. We are committed to expert installation, strict safety practices, and quality control, ensuring customer satisfaction and lasting value in every project we undertake.
